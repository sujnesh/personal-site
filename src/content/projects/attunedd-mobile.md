## Overview

Attunedd Mobile is the client-side companion to the adaptive coaching backend. The app explores
how onboarding, workout guidance, and progress feedback should feel when AI is part of the product
surface from the start.

## Focus Areas

- Conversational onboarding.
- Workout guidance that feels timely.
- Mobile flows that keep the user training instead of configuring.

## Design Constraint

Fitness products can become dashboards very quickly. This app is a reminder to keep the interface
focused on the next useful action.
