I am keeping the stack intentionally lightweight:

- **Astro** for content-first pages and fast performance
- **Markdown** for writing posts without friction
- **Namecheap + static hosting** for simple domain management and deployment

This setup gives me enough flexibility to grow while keeping maintenance low.
