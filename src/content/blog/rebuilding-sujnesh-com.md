This site is being rebuilt around the work that is actually active: AI systems, training products,
developer tools, and the notes that come from shipping them.

The goal is not to turn it into a heavy portfolio. The goal is simpler: make the homepage useful,
make the work index honest, and keep the writing surface small enough that it stays maintained.

For now, the important change is removing generic starter content and replacing it with real
project entries. Better screenshots, longer case studies, and deeper technical writeups can come
after the structure is worth keeping.
