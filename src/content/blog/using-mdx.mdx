I keep this site updated with a simple workflow:

1. Write ideas in plain Markdown drafts.
2. Promote finished drafts into `src/content/blog/`.
3. Keep each post short and practical.

## Publishing Rules

- Every post should teach one concrete thing.
- Every post should include a next step.
- Every month should have at least one reflection post.

## Why This Works

This process keeps writing lightweight and consistent.

Over time, consistency compounds into a useful archive.
