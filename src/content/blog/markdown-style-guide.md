Before I publish, I run through this short checklist:

1. Is the title specific and useful?
2. Is the opening clear about who this post helps?
3. Did I remove filler and keep practical details?
4. Is there one concrete takeaway at the end?
5. Did I add links or references where needed?

## Why I Keep A Checklist

Writing feels easier when quality checks are repeatable.

This prevents over-editing and helps me publish consistently.
